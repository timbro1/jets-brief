Jets Brief — Issue #3

Helly still gone, room says move on, Monday deadline looms

Week of September 22–28, 2026 · Independent Jets fan coverage

Connor Hellebuyck is still suspended, still a Jet, and still untraded. Local coverage this morning framed the next step as moving on: Adam Lowry on the pieces who showed up, Kyle Connor saying a half-in return would be a rough walk into the room, and beat writers floating a waiver claim behind Stuart Skinner before the 4 p.m. CT roster deadline. Preseason is done. Opening night is Friday. The Helly file did not close the gap.

What happened

The week on the ice closed with two more exhibition answers and a 1-2-1 preseason record. Tuesday at Canada Life, Edmonton won 2-1 in overtime. Alex Iafallo banked the only Jets goal off a Nino Niederreiter board carom. Dom DiVincentiis made 21 saves. Atro Leppänen tied it late in the first; Viljami Marjala beat DiVincentiis over the glove at 1:26 of OT after a Jets line change. Challenges were not available that night, so Winnipeg could not challenge Leppänen’s goal for goaltender interference.

Friday in Denver finished the slate: Avalanche 5, Jets 2. Winnipeg led 2-1 in the second on a Niederreiter power-play rebound and a Vlad Namestnikov finish before Colorado scored four unanswered. T.J. Hughes had three for Colorado (empty-netter included). Cale Makar beat Skinner under the glove late. Skinner stopped 28. Scott Arniel’s takeaway was summer-hockey mode, timing, and details — not panic over exhibition standings. Colorado Springs practice and bonding followed, then home.

Roster work was the weekend story. CONFIRMED: Friday’s waiver group (Isaak Phillips, Nikita Chibrikov, Parker Ford, Noah Gregor) cleared and can report to the Manitoba Moose. CONFIRMED Sunday (Illegal Curve citing Ken Wiebe): Danny Zhilkin and Henry Thrun went on waivers for Moose assignment. JetsNation reported Thomas Milic was loaned to the Moose and that DiVincentiis is the backup behind Skinner heading into Monday’s 23-man deadline. Elias Salomonsson (shoulder) and Garrett Brown remain hurt; Salomonsson’s return talk has shifted toward mid-October. Illegal Curve’s Monday Morning Papers had Winnipeg at 24 healthy bodies after the Thrun/Zhilkin moves, needing one more cut by 4 p.m. CT, and watching whether the club claims a goalie when the waiver wire hits.

The Helly file stayed stuck on the September 17 baseline. He remains suspended for failing to report. No CONFIRMED trade. Canadian Press (Jeff Hamilton), carried by CBC and Global/Globe framing, had Lowry: “We still have a lot of good pieces,” and Connor on a return without full recommitment: “that’s going to be a pretty rough situation to walk into, to be honest.” Destination chatter from the week (Sharks colour via Friedman on radio, Sabres injury-wrinkle speculation earlier) is RUMOUR / insider colour only — no named Jets deal, no landing spot.

Today’s crease side-debate is the backup. Free Press / Illegal Curve morning links asked whether another goalie is on the wish list. Mike McIntyre noted 11 goalies on waivers Sunday and would not be surprised by a Jets claim Monday, floating names that catch the eye (Dylan Garand, Clay Stevenson, Laurent Brossoit, David Rittich). That is beat speculation, not a completed claim. Label it RUMOUR until the transaction board lights up.

What it means

Opening night is Friday, October 2 vs Boston at Canada Life Centre. Skinner (two-year, $7.5M US deal from July 1, per Canadian Press) is the starter by process of elimination and by camp work. DiVincentiis looks like the internal backup unless Chevy actually claims someone with NHL starts. That is not a Vezina tandem on paper. It is a playable one if the skaters clean up the second-period turnovers Arniel flagged in Denver and if special teams land anywhere near the top-10 band Josh Morrissey talked about at camp.

The Zhilkin cut will annoy the prospect wing — Illegal Curve said as much — because he looked useful on a fourth line and PK. Keeping Niederreiter and Namestnikov for night one is a win-now call after a 35-35-12 miss. Camp line chatter still disagrees on who rides with Scheifele and where Björck, Rosén, Lambert, and Yager land. Wait for the submitted 23. St. Ivany vs Fleury next to Mario Ferraro is the blue-line watch until Salomonsson is healthy.

On Helly, Chevy’s line still governs: no fair-value offers yet, no timeline, door not closed, choice to play here rests with the goaltender. Connor’s quote matters more than another anonymous destination tweet. If Hellebuyck comes back half-in, the room already told you how that lands. If he stays out, Skinner’s workload is the real October story — and whether True North adds a waiver veteran so DiVincentiis is not the only insurance policy.

What to watch next 7 days

  1. Monday 4 p.m. CT roster deadline — Official 23-man list, whether Zhilkin/Thrun clear, and whether Winnipeg claims a goalie (Brossoit and friends are names in the air, not transactions).
  2. Friday, October 2 vs Boston at Canada Life — First night that counts. Skinner’s real opener, special-teams first look, Arniel’s “details” speech against a Bruins team that will not gift soft preseason minutes.
  3. Any Helly movement — Fair-value package clearing Dreger’s bar, Petkau statement, or a genuine return conversation. Silence remains the baseline.
  4. Salomonsson timeline — Mid-October return talk vs another slip; Ferraro’s third-pair partner until then.
